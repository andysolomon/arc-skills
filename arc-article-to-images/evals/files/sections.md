# Listening machines

Synthetic evaluation article about converting audio into useful representations.

## Capture

Begin with a microphone signal and preserve its sample-rate metadata.

### Sampling

A sample is a measurement at a particular instant.

### Filtering

Filter out frequencies above the new usable range before downsampling.

## Interpret

Choose an interpretation task for the captured signal.

### Speech

Transcription produces text from speech.

### Speech

Speaker verification evaluates a speaker identity claim. It is a different task from transcription.
